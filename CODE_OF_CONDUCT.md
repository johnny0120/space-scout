# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Space Scout welcoming, respectful, and free
from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on
what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language, and other
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's configured GitHub
security or private-contact channel. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled
fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
